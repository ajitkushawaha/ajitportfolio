4. PROJECT EXPLANATION FORMAT

Use this structure for every project:

1. What was the problem?
2. What did I build?
3. What was my role?
4. What tech stack did I use?
5. What was one technical challenge?
6. What was the result?

Do not explain randomly. Always follow this structure.

This project shows my ability to build a real product around a business workflow, not just a portfolio demo.
